🚀  JUX MISSION CONTROL  🚀
========================

Jux has no garbage collector. It has reference counting, which is
  a garbage collector that counts on its fingers.  🗑️

`if cfg` runs at compile time, `if` runs at run time, and a branch
  that is never built is the fastest code you will ever write.  📖

There is one `print`. It always ends the line. There is no second
  `print` that does not, which is a story for another release.  🤔

`final` is immutable and `var` is mutable. The compiler will remind
  you of this. Warmly. Repeatedly.  💛

Pre-1.0 means the language is younger than some of its bug reports,
  and moves faster than all of them.  🏃
