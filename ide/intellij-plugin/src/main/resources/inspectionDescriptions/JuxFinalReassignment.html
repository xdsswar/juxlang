Reports a store into a final or const binding (E0464).

A final or const parameter, local, for init variable or for-each binder (for (final String? note : notes)) is immutable. A plain x = e, a compound x += e and x++ / --x on it are all errors, exactly as the compiler reports them.

A final ref or weak binding is not reported: there x = v stores through the shared cell rather than rebinding the name. A plain binding of the same name in an inner scope shadows the final one and may be assigned freely.

Quick-fix: drop the final / const keyword from the declaration.
