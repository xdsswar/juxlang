Reports a checked exception that is thrown, or declared by a called method, but neither caught nor declared in the enclosing method's throws clause.

An exception class is checked when its extends chain reaches Exception without passing RuntimeException. The IDE follows the chain through the project's own declarations only, so exceptions declared elsewhere are left to the compiler. Nothing is reported inside a lambda.

Quick-fixes: add the exception to the method's throws, or surround the statement with try / catch.
